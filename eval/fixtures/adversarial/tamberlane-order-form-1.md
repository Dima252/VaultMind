# Tamberlane Order Form No. 1

Order Form No. 1, dated March 3, 2025, issued by Quarrystone Robotics Ltd. ("Buyer") to Tamberlane Sensors Inc. ("Seller") for the supply of thermal sensor modules.

## Parties

Seller: Tamberlane Sensors Inc., 140 Corvel Street, Ashbury Flats, Colorado 80999. Contact: Leopold Farrant, Sales Director, orders@tamberlane.example.com.

Buyer: Quarrystone Robotics Ltd., 3 Millrace Court, Dunholt, Colorado 80998. Contact: Imogen Vasquez-Hale, Head of Supply Chain, purchasing@quarrystone.example.com.

## Order Details

Product: TS-40 thermal sensor module, datasheet revision D.

Quantity: 5,000 units for the first delivery, with further call-offs as needed to meet Buyer's annual volume of 20,000 units.

Unit price: USD 19.75 per TS-40 thermal sensor module, exclusive of taxes and shipping.

Total for the first delivery: USD 98,750, exclusive of taxes and shipping.

## Delivery

Delivery terms: FCA Seller's warehouse, Ashbury Flats, Colorado.

Requested delivery date: April 15, 2025.

Packaging: anti-static trays of 50 units, 20 trays per carton, cartons labeled with Buyer's part number QR-TS40.

## Payment

Payment terms: net thirty (30) days from the invoice date, by bank transfer.

## Quality

Each shipment shall include a certificate of conformance and the lot numbers of the modules supplied. Buyer will perform incoming inspection on a sample of each lot and report any nonconformities to Seller within ten (10) business days of receipt.

## Changes and Cancellation

Buyer may change the requested delivery date or reduce the quantity of an undelivered call-off by written notice to Seller at least fourteen (14) days before the scheduled dispatch date. Modules already dispatched cannot be cancelled. Seller will confirm any change in writing within three (3) business days.

## Returns

Nonconforming modules reported within the inspection period will be collected by Seller at its own cost and replaced in the next shipment, or credited against Buyer's next invoice at Buyer's choice.
