# Penhallow Studios Content License Agreement

This Content License Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Penhallow Studios Ltd., a production company with its registered office at 8 Corriemoor Yard, Thessingham, Maine 04999 ("Licensor"), and Grisedale Broadcast Network Inc., a broadcaster with its principal office at 600 Lantern Quay, Halverton, New York 10999 ("Licensee").

## Article 1. Definitions

1.1 "Licensed Content" means the documentary series titled "Tides of the Northern Coast" (eight episodes of approximately fifty minutes each), together with the trailers, stills and promotional materials listed in Exhibit A.

1.2 "Territory" means the United States of America and Canada.

1.3 "Net Receipts" means all gross amounts actually received by Licensee from the exploitation of the Licensed Content, less sales taxes, refunds and third-party distribution fees not exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of gross amounts.

## Article 2. Grant of License

2.1 License. Licensor grants Licensee an exclusive license in the Territory to broadcast, stream and make available on demand the Licensed Content during the License Period, through Licensee's own television channels and subscription streaming service.

2.2 Reserved Rights. All rights not expressly granted to Licensee are reserved to Licensor, including theatrical, airline, educational and home video rights.

2.3 Sublicensing. Licensee may not sublicense the Licensed Content to any third party without Licensor's prior written consent.

## Article 3. License Period

3.1 Term. The license period begins on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years (the "License Period").

3.2 Holdback. During the first twelve (12) months of the License Period, Licensor shall not license the Licensed Content to any other broadcaster or streaming service in the Territory.

## Article 4. Delivery

4.1 Licensor shall deliver the Licensed Content in high-definition digital files meeting the technical specifications in Exhibit B no later than March 1, 2025.

4.2 Licensee shall notify Licensor of any technical defects in the delivered materials within thirty (30) days of delivery, and Licensor shall supply replacement materials within fifteen (15) days of such notice.

## Article 5. License Fee and Royalties

5.1 Minimum Guarantee. Licensee shall pay Licensor a non-refundable minimum guarantee of USD 240,000, payable in two equal installments: the first on signature of this Agreement and the second on acceptance of delivery of the Licensed Content. The minimum guarantee is an advance against royalties.

5.2 Royalty Rate. Licensee shall pay Licensor a royalty equal to seven and one-half percent (7.5%) of Net Receipts derived from the exploitation of the Licensed Content, once the royalties earned exceed the minimum guarantee.

5.3 Statements and Payment. Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Licensee shall deliver to Licensor a royalty statement showing Net Receipts for that quarter and shall pay the royalties due for that quarter.

6.1 Licensee shall display the Licensor's on-screen credits as delivered and shall not edit the Licensed Content except for time-segment editing, commercial breaks and compliance with broadcast standards.

6.2 Licensee may use excerpts of up to three (3) minutes per episode for promotion of its broadcasts of the Licensed Content.

7.1 Licensor warrants that it owns or controls all rights in the Licensed Content necessary to grant the license, and that the Licensed Content does not infringe the rights of any third party or contain defamatory material.

7.2 Licensor shall indemnify Licensee against third-party claims arising from a breach of the warranties in Section 7.1.

## Article 8. Limitation of Liability

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, special or consequential damages. Except for the indemnity in Section 7.2, each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid and payable under Article 5.

## Article 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice.

9.2 Licensor may terminate this Agreement on written notice if Licensee fails to pay any amount within fifteen (15) days after written notice of non-payment.

9.3 On expiry or termination, all rights in the Licensed Content revert to Licensor and Licensee shall remove the Licensed Content from its services within ten (10) days.
