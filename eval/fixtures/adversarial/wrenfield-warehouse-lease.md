# Wrenfield Warehouse Lease

This Lease (the "Lease") is made as of February 1, 2025 between Wrenfield Property Partners LP, located at 18 Gallowfield Crescent, Tarrowby, Georgia 30999 ("Landlord"), and Sallowmere Freight Co., located at 260 Brackenhurst Road, Tarrowby, Georgia 30998 ("Tenant").

## 1. Premises

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant the warehouse known as Unit 4, Wrenfield Logistics Park, Tarrowby, Georgia, comprising approximately 42,000 square feet of warehouse space, 3,500 square feet of office space and the right to use six loading docks (the "Premises").

## 2. Use

2.1 Tenant shall use the Premises only for the storage and distribution of packaged goods and related office purposes, and shall not store hazardous materials at the Premises.

## 3. Term and Renewal

3.1 Term. The initial term of this Lease begins on March 1, 2025 and ends on February 28, 2027.

3.2 Renewal. The Term shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless Tenant gives Landlord written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current Term.

## 4. Rent

4.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay base rent of USD 31,500 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month.

4.2 Escalation. Base rent increases by three percent (3%) at the start of each renewal period.

4.3 Operating Costs. Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of real estate taxes, insurance and common area maintenance costs, estimated monthly and reconciled annually.

## 5. Security Deposit

5.1 Tenant shall deliver a security deposit of USD 63,000 on signature of this Lease. Landlord shall return the deposit, less any amounts applied to Tenant's unpaid obligations, within thirty (30) days after the end of the Lease.

## 6. Maintenance

6.1 Landlord is responsible for the roof, structure and exterior walls. Tenant is responsible for the interior of the Premises, the dock doors and the heating and cooling systems serving the office space.

## 7. Default

7.1 If Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice that it is overdue, Landlord may terminate this Lease and recover possession of the Premises.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.

Executed by Landlord, by its general partner, Corwin Ashdale, and by Tenant, by its President, Marguerite Fennimore.
