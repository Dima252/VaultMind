# Ferncliff Equipment Maintenance Agreement

This Equipment Maintenance Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of January 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Ferncliff Facilities Group Inc., located at 52 Ostley Road, Granmere, Minnesota 55999 ("Contractor"), and Halberd Cold Storage LLC, located at 9 Wharfside Terrace, Pellingford, Minnesota 55998 ("Client").

## 1. Services

1.1 Contractor shall provide preventive maintenance, inspection and emergency repair services for the refrigeration compressors, condensers and loading dock doors at Client's Pellingford warehouse (the "Equipment"), as described in Schedule 1.

1.2 Preventive maintenance visits shall take place once per month on a date agreed with Client's site manager.

1.3 Contractor shall respond to emergency service calls within four (4) hours, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years. It may be renewed by written agreement of the parties.

## 3. Fees

3.1 Monthly Fee. Client shall pay Contractor a fixed monthly service fee of USD 12,500, payable in advance on the first business day of each month.

3.2 Parts. Replacement parts are not included in the monthly fee and are charged at Contractor's cost plus ten percent (10%), as shown on each service report.

3.3 Invoices for parts are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

## 4. Standards

4.1 Contractor shall perform the services in a good and workmanlike manner, using qualified technicians and in compliance with the Equipment manufacturers' recommendations.

## 5. Insurance and Liability

5.1 Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 2,000,000 per occurrence for the duration of this Agreement.

5.2 Contractor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months before the claim, except for bodily injury or damage to property caused by Contractor's negligence.

## 6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice, or immediately on written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days.

## 7. Governing Law

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
