# Amendment No. 1 to the Ferncliff Equipment Maintenance Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "Amendment") is made on June 2, 2025 between Ferncliff Facilities Group Inc. ("Contractor") and Halberd Cold Storage LLC ("Client"), and amends the Equipment Maintenance Agreement between them dated January 1, 2025 (the "Agreement").

## Background

A. Client has added a second cold storage chamber at its Pellingford warehouse, which increases the number of refrigeration compressors covered by the Agreement from six to nine.

B. The parties wish to extend the services to the additional Equipment and to adjust the monthly fee accordingly.

## Amendments

1. Additional Equipment. Schedule 1 of the Agreement is amended to include the three refrigeration compressors installed in the new chamber, serial numbers FC-7781, FC-7782 and FC-7783.

2. Amendment to Section 3.1. Effective July 1, 2025, the fixed monthly service fee set out in Section 3.1 of the Agreement is increased from USD 12,500 to USD 14,200. The fee remains payable in advance on the first business day of each month.

3. Emergency Response. The emergency response time in Section 1.3 of the Agreement is unchanged.

4. Commissioning Visit. Contractor shall carry out a one-time commissioning inspection of the three additional compressors before July 1, 2025 at no additional charge, and shall deliver a written report to Client's site manager within five (5) business days of the visit.

5. Service Reports. From the date of this Amendment, Contractor's monthly service reports shall list each compressor by serial number, together with the parts replaced and the hours worked on it during the month.

## General

6. Except as amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

7. In case of conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment prevails.

Signed by the authorized representatives of the parties.
