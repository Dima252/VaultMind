# Kestrelpoint Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the Payroll Services Agreement dated May 5, 2025 (the "Principal Agreement") between Ambervale Dental Group PC, a professional corporation located at 31 Orrin Mews, Leddington, Oregon 97999 ("Controller"), and Kestrelpoint Payroll Services LLC, a limited liability company located at 905 Brightfold Way, Suite 12, Cassary, Oregon 97998 ("Processor").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that Processor processes on behalf of Controller under the Principal Agreement, including employee names, addresses, tax identifiers, bank account details and salary information.

1.2 "Personal Data Breach" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

1.3 "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of Controller.

## 2. Scope and Instructions

2.1 Processor shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Controller, including the instructions set out in the Principal Agreement and this DPA, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law.

2.2 The subject matter of the processing is the calculation and payment of wages, the preparation of payroll tax filings and the issuance of payslips to Controller's employees for the duration of the Principal Agreement.

2.3 Processor shall promptly inform Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction from Controller infringes applicable data protection law.

## 3. Confidentiality of Personnel

3.1 Processor shall ensure that every person it authorizes to process Personal Data is bound by a written duty of confidentiality and receives appropriate data protection training at least once a year.

## 4. Security Measures

4.1 Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including encryption of Personal Data at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, role-based access controls and logging of access to payroll records.

4.2 Processor shall test and evaluate the effectiveness of these measures at least annually and shall provide Controller with a summary of the results on request.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 Controller authorizes Processor to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 2. Processor shall give Controller at least thirty (30) days' written notice before adding or replacing a Sub-processor, during which Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

5.2 Processor shall impose on each Sub-processor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA and remains fully liable to Controller for the performance of each Sub-processor.

## 6. Assistance

6.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Processor shall assist Controller in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability.

6.2 Processor shall forward to Controller within five (5) business days any request it receives directly from a data subject relating to Personal Data.

## 7. Personal Data Breach

7.1 Notification. Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data processed under this DPA.

7.2 Content of the Notice. The notice shall describe the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences of the breach, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its possible adverse effects.

## 8. Audits

8.1 Processor shall make available to Controller all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for audits, including inspections, by Controller or an independent auditor mandated by Controller, on at least thirty (30) days' notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, except following a Personal Data Breach.

## 9. Return and Deletion

9.1 Upon termination or expiry of the Principal Agreement, Processor shall, at Controller's choice, return all Personal Data to Controller or securely delete it within thirty (30) days, and shall delete existing copies unless applicable law requires their retention.

9.2 Processor shall certify the deletion in writing on Controller's request.

## 10. Liability and Precedence

10.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Principal Agreement.

10.2 In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Principal Agreement regarding the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

## 11. Governing Law

11.1 This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, and the courts of Multnomah County, Oregon have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
