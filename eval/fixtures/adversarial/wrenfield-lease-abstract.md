# Wrenfield Warehouse Lease Abstract

Lease abstract for Unit 4, Wrenfield Logistics Park, prepared by Tarrowby Property Management for the Landlord's portfolio records. Prepared on March 10, 2025.

## Parties

Landlord: Wrenfield Property Partners LP, 18 Gallowfield Crescent, Tarrowby, Georgia 30999.

Tenant: Sallowmere Freight Co., 260 Brackenhurst Road, Tarrowby, Georgia 30998. Tenant contact: Marguerite Fennimore, President, facilities@sallowmere.example.com.

## Premises

Unit 4, Wrenfield Logistics Park: approximately 42,000 square feet of warehouse space and 3,500 square feet of office space, with six loading docks.

Permitted use: storage and distribution of packaged goods, with related office use. No hazardous materials.

## Key Dates

Lease date: February 1, 2025.

Commencement date: March 1, 2025.

Expiration of initial term: February 28, 2027.

Renewal: automatic renewal for successive one-year periods.

Non-renewal notice: Tenant must give written notice at least sixty (60) days before the end of the current term.

## Financial Terms

Base rent: USD 31,500 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month.

Escalation: three percent (3%) at the start of each renewal period.

Operating costs: Tenant pays its proportionate share of taxes, insurance and common area maintenance, estimated monthly and reconciled annually.

Security deposit: USD 63,000, returnable within thirty (30) days after the end of the lease, less amounts applied to unpaid obligations.

## Responsibilities

Landlord: roof, structure and exterior walls.

Tenant: interior, dock doors, and heating and cooling for the office space.

## Default

Landlord may terminate if rent remains unpaid ten (10) days after written notice that it is overdue.

## Notes

Governing law: State of Georgia. Abstract prepared for internal portfolio tracking.

Portfolio actions: add the renewal notice deadline to the asset management calendar, confirm the operating cost estimate with the Tenant before the first annual reconciliation, and schedule a walk-through of the loading docks with the Tenant's facilities team before the end of the first lease year.

Distribution: asset management, accounts receivable and the on-site facilities coordinator for Wrenfield Logistics Park.
