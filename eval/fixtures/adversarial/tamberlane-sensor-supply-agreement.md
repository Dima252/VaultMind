# Tamberlane Sensor Supply Agreement

This Sensor Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated March 3, 2025 and is made between Tamberlane Sensors Inc., located at 140 Corvel Street, Ashbury Flats, Colorado 80999 ("Seller"), and Quarrystone Robotics Ltd., located at 3 Millrace Court, Dunholt, Colorado 80998 ("Buyer").

## 1. Products

1.1 Seller shall sell to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, the TS-40 thermal sensor module and any related accessories that Buyer orders under this Agreement (the "Products").

1.2 Each TS-40 thermal sensor module shall conform to Seller's published datasheet revision D.

## 2. Ordering

2.1 Buyer shall order Products by written purchase order. Seller shall confirm or reject each purchase order within three (3) business days.

2.2 Buyer shall purchase at least 20,000 TS-40 thermal sensor modules in each calendar year of the term.

## 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement has a term of two (2) years from the date above.

## 4. Pricing and Payment

4.1 Unit Price. The price for each TS-40 thermal sensor module is USD 18.40 per unit, exclusive of taxes and shipping.

4.2 Seller shall invoice each shipment on dispatch. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Seller shall deliver Products FCA Seller's warehouse in Ashbury Flats, Colorado, within twenty-one (21) days of confirming each purchase order.

## 6. Warranty

6.1 Seller warrants that the Products will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for twelve (12) months from delivery. Seller shall repair or replace defective Products at its own cost.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Seller's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts paid by Buyer in the twelve (12) months before the claim.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

Signed for Tamberlane Sensors Inc. by Leopold Farrant, Sales Director, and for Quarrystone Robotics Ltd. by Imogen Vasquez-Hale, Head of Supply Chain.
